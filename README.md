# Strategic Procurement Transformation

This project utilizes the **DMAIC framework** and **Power BI** to identify, analyze, and mitigate revenue leakage within procurement operations.

---

### 1. Project Scoping & Baseline (Define Phase)
In the Define phase, I established the project boundaries and identified a baseline Sigma level of **3.27**, tracking a total of **$98.22M** in revenue leakage.

![Executive Summary](1_Executive_Summary.jpg)

---

### 2. Root Cause Discovery (Analyze Phase)
Using the **80/20 Rule**, this phase pinpoints high-frequency defect concentrations. The analysis revealed that "Casual Wear" and "T-shirts" were the primary drivers, accounting for over **60%** of total revenue loss.

![Root Cause](2_Root_Cause_Analysis.jpg)

---

### 3. Financial Optimization (Improve Phase)
The Improve phase features a dynamic **"What-If" simulation** designed to optimize profit buffers. This model projects a potential revenue recovery of **$137.31M** based on adjusted operational parameters.

![Impact Simulation](3_Profit_Simulation.jpg)

---

## Technical Highlights
* **Framework:** Six Sigma DMAIC
* **KPIs:** DPMO, Process Yield %, Sigma Level, Revenue Leakage
* **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
