# Strategic Procurement Transformation (DMAIC Analysis)

## Project Objective
[cite_start]To identify and mitigate revenue leakage in procurement operations using the Six Sigma DMAIC framework[cite: 1, 2].

## Key Business Metrics
* [cite_start]**Total Revenue Analyzed:** $142M [cite: 11]
* [cite_start]**Total Identified Leakage:** $98.22M [cite: 2, 13]
* [cite_start]**Baseline Sigma Level:** 3.27 [cite: 2, 39]
* [cite_start]**Process Yield:** 22.60% [cite: 2, 52]
* [cite_start]**Target Sigma:** 5.22 [cite: 12]

## Analysis & Insights (Analyze Phase)
* [cite_start]**Root Cause:** Applied the **80/20 Rule** to discover that **Casual Wear** (specifically T-shirts and Jeans) accounts for over **60% of total leakage**[cite: 2, 89].
* [cite_start]**Top Leakage Driver:** T-shirts alone contributed **$19.6M** in lost revenue[cite: 80].

## Simulated Impact (Improve Phase)
* [cite_start]**Projected Recovery:** Through profit buffer optimization, we projected a revenue recovery of **$137.31M**[cite: 2].
* [cite_start]**Simulated Revenue:** $148.64M[cite: 137].

## Governance (Control Phase)
* [cite_start]**Audit Trail:** Implemented a real-time tracking system to monitor **Defect Hot Spots** by Receipt Number (e.g., BL94, LN25) to ensure process health[cite: 2, 160].
